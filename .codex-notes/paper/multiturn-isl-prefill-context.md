# Multi-Turn ISL vs New Prefill Context

Date: 2026-04-30

## Key Distinction

For multi-turn serving, there are two different token lengths that should not be conflated:

- **Full ISL / total context tokens**: the full prompt/context length submitted on a turn.
- **New prefill tokens**: the incremental uncached suffix that must be prefetched after prefix-cache reuse.

Under the normal serving definition, **ISL means full context length**, and it generally increases over turns because the conversation history grows.

However, under prefix caching, later turns do not pay prefill cost for the full cumulative ISL. They mostly pay for the small new suffix.

In short:

> Full ISL increases over turns. New prefill usually drops after turn 1 for agentic workloads.

## Evidence From Extracted Distributions

These numbers come from `inference-benchmark/data/distributions/*.json`, generated by `inference-benchmark/scripts/build_trace_distributions.py`.

### Full ISL Trend

| Workload | Turn 0 median full ISL | Later median full ISL trend | Decreases? |
|---|---:|---|---|
| SWE-bench multi-turn | 1,032 | 1,092 -> 1,796 -> 2,937 by turn 10 | No |
| TerminalBench multi-turn | 980 | 1,048 -> 1,555 -> 2,325 by turn 10 | No |
| Chat multi-turn | 90 | 377 -> 1,342 -> 1,626 by turn 10 | No |
| OSWorld multi-turn | 1,241 | noisy, roughly flat/growing | Some decreases; trace/estimator caveat |

### New Prefill Trend

| Workload | Turn 0 median new prefill | Later median new prefill |
|---|---:|---:|
| SWE-bench multi-turn | 1,032 | ~57-66 |
| TerminalBench multi-turn | 980 | ~57-78 |
| Chat multi-turn | 90 | ~170-300 early |
| OSWorld multi-turn | 1,241 | tiny/noisy; trace/estimator caveat |

## Interpretation

This supports the cache-aware serving story:

- Agentic traces can have long cumulative context.
- Decode attention still sees the full KV length.
- TTFT prefill should be modeled using the newly appended suffix, not cumulative ISL, when prefix caching is effective.
- This is why full-prefill prediction overestimates TerminalBench/SWE-bench multi-turn TTFT.

Chat behaves differently:

- Chat multi-turn has much smaller full contexts.
- First turn is not especially large.
- Early turns add relatively balanced user/assistant content, so new prefill remains larger than SWE-bench/TerminalBench deltas.

OSWorld needs caution:

- The extracted OSWorld artifact shows context decreases / non-growth turns.
- That likely means the raw trace format or coarse token estimator is not a clean literal prefix-growing prompt sequence.
- OSWorld should not be trusted for precise new-prefill distributions until the trace extraction is audited.

## Paper Wording Candidate

Multi-turn agentic workloads separate cumulative context from incremental prefill. Across SWE-bench and TerminalBench traces, median full context grows with turn depth, while median newly-prefilled tokens after the first turn remain only tens of tokens. Thus, later turns decode over long KV caches but pay prefill cost only for a small suffix when prefix caching is effective. This explains why cumulative-ISL full-prefill models overestimate multi-turn TTFT and motivates cache-aware serving prediction.
